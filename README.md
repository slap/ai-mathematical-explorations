# AI-Generated Mathematical Explorations

Mathematical claims, arguments and possible proofs produced by AI systems in the course of
research work, published **unverified** so that others can inspect, check, refute or develop them.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Inclusion in this repository is not a claim of correctness. Unless an exploration is
> explicitly marked otherwise, nobody has verified it.

These explorations were generated by AI systems in the context of research activities of
Santiago Laplagne at the Instituto de Cálculo, Universidad de Buenos Aires. He provides the
research context, the problems and the questions; the mathematical content is produced by the
AI. He does not claim authorship of the mathematical results generated by the AI systems and
has not independently verified all mathematical claims.

## Status

| | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 🟠 **Unverified** | Published as generated. No independent check on record. |
| 🟡 **Partially verified** | Parts of the argument have been checked and documented; gaps remain. |
| 🟢 **Verified** | A complete mathematical check is on record, by a named reviewer or a proof assistant. |
| 🔴 **Refuted** | A documented error or counterexample invalidates the main claim. |
| ⚪ **Superseded** | Replaced by a later exploration, which is linked from the page. |

Every status other than *Unverified* points to the issue or document that justifies it.
The criteria are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md#verification-status).

## What this repository is

- An archive of mathematical material generated by AI systems, kept in the form in which it
  was produced, with its provenance (model, date, prompt, conversation context).
- An invitation: these arguments look interesting enough to be worth someone's time, and
  checking them is exactly what this repository asks for.
- A record of what happens to each exploration afterwards — verification, error, prior work.

## What this repository is not

- Not a collection of results whose correctness has been certified by Santiago Laplagne, or
  by anyone else.
- Not a set of papers authored by him. He selected and published the material; he did not
  produce the mathematics and does not claim it.
- Not a journal, a preprint server or a peer review system.
- Not a claim of novelty. Some of this may already be in the literature, or may be wrong.

## Taking part

Everything happens through [issues](../../issues):

| If you | Open |
| --- | --- |
| checked an argument, in whole or in part | [Verification / mathematical review](../../issues/new?template=01-verification.yml) |
| found an error or a counterexample | [Counterexample / error](../../issues/new?template=02-error.yml) |
| know the result, or something close, from the literature | [Related work](../../issues/new?template=03-related-work.yml) |
| want to discuss anything else | [Discussion](../../issues/new?template=04-discussion.yml) |

A verification does not have to be complete to be useful: a checked step, a failed special
case or a missing hypothesis all change what the next reader should look at.

## Layout

Each exploration is self-contained in its own directory:

```
explorations/001-.../
    README.md        what it claims, its status, its provenance
    metadata.yml     structured record, used to build the index above
    exploration.pdf  the document
    source.tex       LaTeX source of the PDF
    ai-output.md     the original AI output, unedited, and the prompts
```

Adding, updating and citing explorations: [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
